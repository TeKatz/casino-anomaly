# Casino Anomaly — Footstep audio

36 mono WAV files, 48 kHz, signed 16-bit PCM (Godot-ready).
6 each: wood_walk, wood_run, carpet_walk, carpet_run; 12: wet_carpet_walk.
Dry clips retain their source dynamics, with short edge fades and PCM conversion.
Wet clips combine filtered carpet steps with cut, mono-converted, filtered and attenuated wet recordings. No wet sprint set: Backrooms movement is walking only.
